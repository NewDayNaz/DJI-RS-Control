Lens calibration

Two steps. First characterize the motion profile: the motor jogs, you say whether the LCD millimetres changed (or type them). That picks min-run, cruise, and accel. Then map millimetres with that same profile. Overlay gone: rocker a hair on the camera to refresh it, then type. Motor stop wrong: power-zoom the rocker, leave the gimbal ring, type.
